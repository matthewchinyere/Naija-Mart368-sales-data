# Naija Mart360 Sales Dashboard  - Excel Project
**Tools:** Excel (PivotTable, Slicers, Timeline, Donut, Bar, Line Charts) | 200 Orders | ** Nov 2024 - Apr 2025 **
## Overview
Analyzed 200 orders generating **₦125.49M Revenue, ₦33.99M Profit (27.1% margin), AOV ₦627.47K** for a Nigerian retail business selling across 5 sales channels and 7 regions.
# Dashboard Features
- **KPIs:** Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value
- **Interactivity:** 4 Slicers (Region, Category, Sales Channel, Payment Channel) + 4 Timelines (Years, Quarters, Months, Days)
- **Charts:** Monthly Sales & Profit Trend, Sales by Category, Top 5 Products, Sales Channel Performance, Payment Method Performance
# Key Insight
 1.	**Extreme Dependency Risk:** Electronics = ₦84.16M (67.1% of revenue). Fashion = ₦2.35M (1.9%). Top 2 SKUs (Samsung 65" QLED TV ₦37.50M + iPhone 15 Pro ₦24.98M) = 49.8% of total revenue.
 2.	**Channel Inefficiency:** Jumia leads at ₦29.56M (23.6%) but WhatsApp is most profitable. Online is weakest at ₦14.61M (11.6%) despite 42 orders — lowest AOV
 3. **Volatility & Regional Imbalance:** April sales crashed to ₦7.50M from ₦20.93M in March (-64%). Ibadan top region ₦25.76M, Abuja lowest ₦4.79M despite being capital.
# Recommendations 
1.	**Bundle to De-risk:** Launch "Home Tech Bundle" (TV + Refrigerator) to push Appliances & Furniture, target Electronics <55% share in 90 days.
2.	**Fix Online Channel:** Add WhatsApp checkout button, highlight Bank Transfer (top payment at ₦36.58M), free delivery >₦500k to lift Online from 11.6% to 20%.
3.	**Reallocate Stock & Investigate April Dip:** Use Timeline slicer to find stock-out cause in April, move 30% Abuja inventory to Ibadan/Kaduna.
